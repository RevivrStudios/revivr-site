A few useful answers

Before you reach out.

What should I include in a support request?

Please include the app name, device model, operating system version, and a short description of the issue. If possible, describe the steps that led to it.

Do I need a headset for the WebXR demos?

No. The demos also have a desktop or mobile experience. Immersive VR is available on compatible headsets and browsers. Explore the demos.

Where can I find an app's privacy policy?

Each product page links to its privacy policy. You can also read our website privacy policy.

Can we collaborate on an accessibility project?

Yes. Tell us about your project, intended users, and the problem you'd like to explore at support@revivrstudios.com.
