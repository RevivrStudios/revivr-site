Lantern Lake

a quiet place to put your attention  ·  back to WebXR

Drag to look from the dock. Tap, click, or pinch to release a lantern.

Make yourself comfortable

Settings apply immediately and stay on this device. In VR, use Comfort & help to adjust them without leaving.

Your moment at the lake

Release a lantern and follow its rise. Your lantern has a turquoise band; other lanterns come from across the lake.

In VR, point your head toward a control and let its ring fill. Moving only your eyes won’t aim it. Small movements are okay. Look away briefly before repeating.

The lantern releases, the musical note toggles music, and the door icon over the boat exits VR. Choose Nearby panel in Comfort to bring every action into a comfortable view.

Outside VR, use Release lantern or Tab to it and press Enter. Drag the scene or use arrow keys while the scene is focused to look around.

A relaxation experience, not a medical treatment.
