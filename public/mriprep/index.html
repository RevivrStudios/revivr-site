Meet the MRI

An MRI is a big camera that takes pictures of the inside of your body. This is a place to meet one first — its shape, its sounds, and the one thing it asks you to do: hold still like a statue.

Nothing here is in a hurry. You choose when each thing happens, and you can always go back.
