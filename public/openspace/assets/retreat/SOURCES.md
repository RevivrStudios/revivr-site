# Retreat materials

Terrain: Poly Haven Grass Path 2, CC0. Local 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal,
and roughness maps, tiled at four metres across the 460-metre landscape.
Broad vertex-color variation supplements the photographic detail at distance.
Terrain color now blends four randomized overlapping texture patches to break
the repeating grid. The horizon ridges are original smooth terrain geometry,
with the same lighting and atmospheric fog as the foreground.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_path_2

Cloud sky: Poly Haven, Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), CC0.
`cloud-sky-4k.hdr` is the original 4096 × 2048 HDR panorama, used only for
the visible drifting sky. The original 1K HDR remains the lighting map and fallback.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
- https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/4k/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky_4k.hdr

Wood Floor and White Plaster 02: Poly Haven, CC0.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
- https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02
- https://polyhaven.com/license

Locally served 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps fetched through the Poly Haven asset API. Architecture and furniture are original metre-scale Three.js geometry. The rejected tree_broadleaf.glb is no longer loaded by the retreat. No new Blendkit assets or account credentials were used.
