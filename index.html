Revivr Studios

Different barriers require different technology.

Revivr Studios develops accessibility technology in spatial computing, WebXR, and assistive robotics — three distinct approaches to helping people interact with the world more independently.

Core idea

Three different approaches. One shared purpose.

Spatial computing, WebXR, and assistive robotics are separate technologies solving different problems.

What connects them isn't the technology — it's the goal: giving people more ways to interact, communicate, and act independently.

Spatial Computing

Interact without relying on traditional controls.

Eye tracking, hand tracking, and spatial interfaces can make computing accessible when conventional input becomes difficult.

WebXR

Make immersive experiences easier to reach.

Browser-based XR can bring experiences to phones, tablets, and headsets without requiring specialized software.
