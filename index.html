Human independence, empowered by spatial computing, robotics, and WebXR.

Three separate technologies, each opening a different door. We work across all three, because what restores independence for one person rarely works for the next.

Founded by an XR engineer with ALS in the family. Four apps shipped on the App Store — and counting.

Three technologies, one person at the center

Three separate tools, not one system. Which one helps depends on the person — a headset for one, a browser link for another, a robot arm for a third. All aimed at the same thing.

WebXR — care that travels as a link.

No headset, no install. Reaches a bedside tablet, a clinic's Quest, or a phone.

Spatial Computing — see & do more from where you are.

Full interaction without fine motor dexterity — from a chair or a bed.

Assistive Robotics — reach beyond arm's length.

The headset becomes a remote body: look, pinch, and a simple robot fetches, moves, adjusts.
