Quiet Space

A glowing companion for a few quiet minutes, in Apple Vision Pro.

Designed for Apple Vision Pro

What Quiet Space does

Mixed Reality Passthrough

Your real room is always visible around the orb. Nothing about Quiet Space asks you to leave your space.

Thirteen Visual Presets

Choose from diverse palettes and personalities like Petals, Aurora, Ink Bloom, and more, switchable instantly without breaking your flow.

Intuitive Interaction

Look at the orb and pinch to start. Sweep a hand through the particles in specific presets to interact naturally with the visuals.

Bring Your Own Music

Import any audio file from your library. A session's length always matches its soundscape — put on a longer or shorter track and the session adapts automatically.

Absolute Privacy

No accounts, no sign-in, no network calls, no analytics. Everything stays entirely on your device.

Quiet Space provides an object you can rest your eyes on, not a dashboard demanding your attention.
