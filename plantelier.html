Plantelier

Beautiful 3D décor for your space.

Place true-to-life plants, bonsai, succulents, and seasonal décor in the room you're actually sitting in — no pots to buy, no soil to spill.

$4.99 on the App Store

A Shelf Full of Life, Anywhere You Look

Plantelier turns your empty corner, sideboard, or shelf into a living display. Browse a growing catalog of true-to-scale plants, bonsai, succulents, flowering arrangements, vases, and holiday trees, and place them exactly where you'd put the real thing — no watering can required.

A Catalog Built for Real Rooms

From a Japanese Black Pine bonsai to a fiddle leaf fig, a shelf of succulents to a vase of cherry blossoms — Plantelier's catalog spans six categories and counting: Plants, Bonsai, Succulents, Flowering, Vases, and Christmas Trees. Every piece is modeled true to scale, so it sits on your shelf the way the real thing would.

Grab It Like It's Really There

Pinch and drag any piece to move it. Two-hand pinch to rotate and resize. Press and hold to bring up a quick menu. Every gesture feels as natural as rearranging a real shelf — because your hands are the only interface.

Room Mode

Ready to go further than a shelf? Room Mode places your décor directly on your real floor, in a full immersive space — with real-world occlusion, so people and furniture pass naturally in front of what you've placed. Arrangements save automatically as you build them.

Know What You're Growing

Every plant comes with a care card: how easy it is to keep alive, how much light and humidity it wants, how often to water it, and whether it's safe to have around pets. Thinking about getting a real fiddle leaf fig? Place one in Plantelier first and find out what you're signing up for.

Built for Vision Pro

Designed specifically for visionOS, Plantelier uses spatial placement and hand-tracking to make every piece feel like it belongs in the room you're standing in — whether that's a studio apartment or a sprawling living room.
