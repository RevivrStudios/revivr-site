Designed for Apple Vision Pro

What Plantelier does

A Shelf Full of Life, Anywhere You Look

Plantelier turns your empty corner, sideboard, or shelf into a living display — placed exactly where you'd put the real thing, no watering can required.

Six Categories and Counting

Plants, Bonsai, Succulents, Flowering, Vases, and Christmas Trees — from a Japanese Black Pine to a fiddle leaf fig, with new items added over time.

Browse, Then Place

Pick from the shelf and drop a piece into your room. New items download in seconds the first time you place them.

Grab It Like It's Really There

Pinch and drag any piece to move it. Two-hand pinch to rotate and resize. Press and hold for a quick menu. Your hands are the only interface.

Room Mode

Go further than a shelf. Room Mode anchors décor to your real floor with real-world occlusion, so people and furniture pass naturally in front of it.

Seasonal, When You Want It

Holiday trees are one category among six — there when December rolls around, out of the way the rest of the year.

Know What You're Growing

Every plant comes with a care card: how easy it is to keep alive, how much light and humidity it wants, how often to water it, and whether it's safe around pets. Thinking about a real fiddle leaf fig? Place one here first and find out what you're signing up for.

Designed specifically for visionOS, Plantelier uses spatial placement and hand-tracking to make every piece feel like it belongs in the room you're standing in — whether that's a studio apartment or a sprawling living room.
