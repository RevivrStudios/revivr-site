Blastra

Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

The short version

Blastra does not collect your personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. It never sends your data anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to send it.

Information we collect

None.

Revivr Studios LLC does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from Blastra. The app contains no analytics software, no advertising software, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use advertising identifiers, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

We operate no servers for Blastra and therefore receive no data from it.

Information stored on your device

Blastra saves your game to your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes your high score, current sector, best sector reached, weapon tier, unlocked ships, total play time, boss-kill count, daily-challenge results, and your settings — difficulty, accessibility options, and controller button mappings.

This information stays on your Apple Vision Pro. It is never uploaded to us and never leaves your device except through Apple's own backup and device-transfer features, which are controlled by your Apple settings and covered by Apple's privacy policy. Deleting Blastra deletes this data.

Game Center

Blastra uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievement progress are submitted to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on leaderboards.

That information is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us. We do not receive your Game Center account details, your real name, or your email address. We see no personally identifying information about you at any point.

If you do not want to participate, you can sign out of Game Center or disable it in your device Settings. Blastra remains fully playable — leaderboards and achievements simply stop being submitted.

Cameras, sensors, and your room

Blastra is a spatial app and places sound and gameplay relative to your surroundings. On Apple Vision Pro this processing is performed by the operating system on your device. The app is never given camera images, video, a scan or map of your room, or any other raw sensor data, and none of that information is recorded, stored, or transmitted by Blastra.

Blastra does not use hand tracking and does not request permission for it. It does not use eye tracking data; visionOS never reveals where you are looking to the app.

Game controllers

Blastra requires a game controller and reads its button and stick input to play the game. This input is used only to control your ship in the moment. It is not logged, stored, or transmitted.

Children

Blastra collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, because we do not collect data from any user.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port the personal data a company holds about you, and to know whether your data is sold.

We hold no personal data about you from Blastra. There is nothing for us to disclose, correct, delete, or port, and we do not and will not sell personal information. Your on-device game data is under your control and is removed when you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Blastra changes how it handles information, we will update this page and revise the effective date above before that version is released. Material changes will be described here rather than made silently.
