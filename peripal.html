PeriPal

Track your symptoms. Walk into every appointment with the data to back you up.

Perimenopause is hard enough. Getting taken seriously shouldn't be.

PeriPal turns a few seconds of daily logging into the evidence you need — a doctor-ready report, a checklist of what to actually ask, and a clear read on whether your treatment is working. Not another journal. An advocate you carry in your pocket.

Every appointment, backed by real data

Weeks of check-ins don't help if they never leave your phone. PeriPal turns them into a report built for a 15-minute appointment: your averages, your most frequent symptoms, and a pre-visit checklist with the specific questions worth asking — pulled from your own patterns, not a generic list. Choose a template for your gynecologist, endocrinologist, or primary care doctor, pick a date range, and export a clean PDF. There's even a line for what your doctor said, so the next report picks up where the last conversation left off.

Logging that fits into a real life

A quick check-in takes seconds: rate how you slept, your mood, your energy, and tap the symptoms — hot flashes, brain fog, irritability, whatever's “usual” for you — right from the Home Screen widget, without even opening the app. Connect Apple Health once, and sleep, steps, resting heart rate, HRV, and cycle data fill in automatically.

Know what's actually working

PeriPal doesn't just log symptoms — it tracks whether what you're trying is helping. Start tracking a new supplement, medication, or habit change, and PeriPal compares your weeks before and after with real discipline, not a same-day guess. Pattern insights surface what's actually linked to what — sleep and mood, stress and hot flashes — so you walk into your next visit with an answer, not just a hunch.

See your patterns, not just your data

Every day gets a plain-language read — calm, steady, mixed, or stormy — measured against your own baseline, not a generic scale. Overlay sleep, mood, energy, mental clarity, resting heart rate, and HRV on one chart to see how they move together. Cycle and flare forecasting give you a heads-up on what the next few days are likely to bring.

Built for the appointment, not just the day of

Turn on Appointment Mode before your next visit and PeriPal counts down for you, automatically preparing your report the day before so you're never scrambling in the waiting room. Afterward, log what your doctor said and what to raise next time — so your history builds instead of resetting every visit.

Grounded in real sources

PeriPal links out to trusted, regularly updated research from The Menopause Society, NHS, NICE, the National Institute on Aging, and the Office on Women's Health — so you're never relying on us alone for the medical picture.

Privacy & Security

Your health data is yours. Reports are generated on your device from your own logs, sync privately through your personal iCloud account, and are never used to train anything or sold to anyone. Apple Health access is entirely optional and revocable at any time.

Free to log. Pro to advocate.

Daily tracking, symptom logging, and the widget are free, always. PeriPal Pro ($29.99/year) unlocks doctor reports, pattern insights, cycle forecasting, and Appointment Mode — the tools built specifically for turning your data into better conversations with your provider.

Built with Empathy

Created by someone who understands the challenges of perimenopause firsthand, PeriPal is designed to support you through this transition with compassion, clarity, and evidence-based insights.
