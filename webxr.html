WebXR

Care that travels as a link.

Build once, access everywhere. Our WebXR solutions work on Vision Pro, Quest, and mobile tablets for patients without headsets.

Why WebXR

An app store is a barrier. A link is not.

WebXR runs therapeutic experiences in an ordinary browser — on a bedside tablet, a clinic's Quest, a phone. Nothing to install, nothing to approve, no account.

Because it lives on the open web, it works with the assistive tech someone already uses: eye-tracking, voice control, adaptive switches. For a person managing ALS, that difference is the whole thing.
