Track Stash

Capture sounds, gear photos, patch settings, notes, and signal chains so you can return to a music idea without losing the setup that made it work.

Designed for Apple Vision Pro, iPad

What Track Stash does

Quick Stash

Record the sound, grab photos of gear, add notes, and keep tweaking without stopping the creative flow.

Your Studio, Organized

Build a reusable library of synths, drum machines, pedals, processors, and studio gear so every session starts from the gear you actually use.

Capture Complete Sessions

Save BPM, key, style, project, gear, audio, photos, mixes, notes, and patch details in one searchable session.

Patch Settings That Make Sense

Use reusable templates for synths, drum machines, pedals, and processors so important settings do not get buried in random notes.

Patterns, Kits, and Performance Details

Document drum machine patterns, voice settings, clock source, sync mode, and notes so a beat can be rebuilt later.

Recall the Setup

Rebuild the signal flow, verify connections, review gear photos, and check patch settings from the session in one focused recall view.

Keep the Work Portable

Back up and restore sessions, gear, templates, chains, patch settings, notes, and attached media.

Record Without Breaking Flow

Capture audio ideas directly into Quick Stash or a full session, then review and organize them when the idea is safe.

Everything Around the Sound

Track Stash connects the musical idea to the gear, settings, media, and notes that explain how it happened.

Track Stash is designed for the moment when a sound is working and you do not want to stop. Capture the idea quickly, then build out the details when you are ready.

Track Stash keeps the creative moment connected to the gear, settings, and media that made it possible.
