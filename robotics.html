Assistive Robotics

The third pillar of our work: simple, affordable robots controlled through the same gaze-and-pinch interactions that make Vision Pro accessible in the first place.

Three verbs

The hardest problems are often small and physical: the cup out of reach, the switch across the room. Look at a thing, pinch, and let a simple machine do the reaching.

Our approach

We are early. Our first bench prototypes pair Vision Pro hand- and eye-tracking with off-the-shelf robotics. No humanoids, no hype — small machines doing small things reliably.

We're looking for collaborators — clinicians, occupational therapists, researchers, families — to shape what gets built first. If that's you, we'd like to talk.
