Privacy Policy for Quiet Space

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Zero Data Collection Policy

Quiet Space is designed with absolute privacy in mind.

  • No Accounts or Sign-ins: The application does not require or allow you to create an account.
  • No Network Calls: The application does not communicate with any external servers. There is no backend infrastructure.
  • No Analytics: We do not track how you use the app, which visual presets you prefer, or how long your sessions last.
  • Local Data Only: Everything — your song imports, your preferences, and your session details — stays entirely on your device. Audio files you import remain securely on your local storage and are never uploaded or analyzed externally.
  • No Eye-Tracking Data Collection: While Quiet Space uses gaze and hand gestures for interactions, no raw eye-tracking or hand-tracking data is ever saved, transmitted, or utilized outside of driving the immediate on-device interactive experience.
